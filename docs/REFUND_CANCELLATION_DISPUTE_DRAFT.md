# Digi-IP Hub — Cancellation, Refund and Payment Dispute Policy Draft

**DRAFT — NOT APPROVED OR PUBLISHED.** Do not present as final customer policy. Obtain qualified legal and finance review before release.

## 1. Assisted payment order
An order can be created before any transfer occurs. A customer-submitted GCash or Maya reference is a claim of payment, not proof of funds. The seller must reconcile the actual merchant/bank transaction independently before approving the order.

## 2. Duplicate, incorrect and unverified transfers
Provide a support channel for duplicate payments, incorrect amounts, wrong destination accounts and references that cannot be matched. Do not request PINs, passwords, one-time codes or wallet credentials. Never promise recovery from a transfer to an unrelated account.

## 3. Pre-activation cancellation
Define when a customer may cancel an unpaid order and how to request cancellation of a submitted order. The technical cancellation/rejection workflow and financial reconciliation must be implemented before publishing timelines or automatic refund promises.

## 4. Refund eligibility
Eligibility, partial-period handling, duplicate payments, mistaken transfers, service non-delivery, technical failure, promotional fees and statutory rights require approved rules. **NO FINAL REFUND ELIGIBILITY OR NON-REFUNDABILITY RULE IS SET BY THIS DRAFT.**

## 5. Processing and disputes
Publish a verified customer support contact, required transaction evidence, response targets, refund method, settlement timeframes, escalation path and record-retention period only after these have been operationally confirmed. Seller review must be auditable and subject to separation of duties where practical.

## 6. Subscription end and renewal
Automatic renewal is not presumed. Define expiry, notice, cancellation cutoff, downgrade, data access after expiration and export/deletion rights in the approved commercial terms.

## 7. Legal compliance
The final policy must respect applicable Philippine consumer protection, electronic commerce, privacy and payment rules. A qualified reviewer should approve the policy and its version/effective date.

**Publication gate:** verified payment channels; supported cancellation/refund process; reconciliation controls; customer contact; approved terms version; business and legal signoff.
