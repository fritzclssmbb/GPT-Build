**DRAFT — NOT FOR PUBLICATION OR CUSTOMER ACCEPTANCE.** Legal and business approval required. No effective date or final price is asserted.

## 1. Parties and service
Digi-IP Hub is a digital profile and controlled-sharing service offered under the FS Softwares brand by TophComm Systems. The seller's full registered legal identity, registered address, contact and tax details must be inserted after verification.

## 2. Plans and fees
A purchase request must identify the selected plan, the total PHP charge and tax treatment, billing period, seat quantity, and any separate setup fees. Only seller-approved values displayed in the purchase flow govern an order. Renewal, upgrade, downgrade and price-change rules are **TO BE APPROVED**.

## 3. Assisted payment
GCash and Maya are proposed assisted-payment methods. An order or customer-submitted transfer reference is not proof of settlement. The seller must independently verify receipt through an authorized channel before activation. Never transfer money to an account not independently confirmed as belonging to the seller.

## 4. Activation and availability
Payment submission does not automatically activate a subscription. Activation occurs only after seller verification and fulfillment of agreed commercial conditions. Activation timelines, support hours, availability commitments and remedies are **TO BE APPROVED**.

## 5. Privacy and user content
Customers control the information they choose to enter and share, subject to product permissions. Collection, hosting, security, retention, disclosure, deletion and data-subject request procedures must be stated in a separately approved privacy notice.

## 6. Cancellation, refunds and disputes
Cancellation rights, billing cutoffs, refund eligibility, processing times, incorrect transfers and dispute escalation are **TO BE APPROVED** and must comply with applicable law. No blanket non-refund clause is established by this draft.

## 7. Responsible use and limitations
Acceptable-use rules, intellectual-property licensing, suspension grounds, liability provisions and dispute-resolution venue are **TO BE APPROVED**.

## 8. Acceptance and records
The system should record the accepted terms version and timestamp with the selected plan, exact charge, billing period and seat count. The seller must preserve an immutable copy or digest of the approved version for future dispute review.
